How it works

1. Read the leaf

The photo is shrunk and scanned pixel by pixel in your browser. Nothing is uploaded. Colours are sorted into healthy green, yellowing, brown, dark, white and orange, and lesion spots are counted.

2. Match to known patterns

Those measurements are compared with the typical look of each disease in the built-in library. Symptoms you tick add extra weight.

3. Advise from an approved source

Advice is a short summary of general extension guidance (ICAR, FAO and state agricultural university advisories). It is not a prescription.

Limits

This is a colour and pattern screening tool, not a laboratory test. Always confirm with your nearest Krishi Vigyan Kendra or agriculture officer before spraying.
